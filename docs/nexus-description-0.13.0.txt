Norse Sense shows you things that Valheim keeps hidden: where mobs can spawn and which pieces need repair. Building helpers included for precise building, freecam building and planting grids, and it even has a cartography table manager.

[b]Client-side, vanilla-balanced and fully customisable.[/b]

[line]
[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Installation[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Mod managers:[/b] automatic install
[*][b]Manual:[/b] put the DLL file in \Steam\steamapps\common\Valheim\BepInEx\plugins
[/list]

[line]
[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Base view[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Spawns:[/b] see where mobs can and can't spawn, on the ground and on the map
[*]An outline around your protected area, nests and spawners marked
[*][b]Raid check:[/b] can raids target you here?
[*][b]Repairs:[/b] damaged pieces glow yellow or red under 50%, pieces with no roof or in water are marked
[*][b]Comfort:[/b] the floor is coloured by how much comfort you'd get standing there, and the best spot for your bed is ringed
[*][b]Map layers:[/b] ward areas (green where you may build, red where you can't), workbench ranges and lines between your portals, with toggles on the large map
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Building[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Fine rotation:[/b] Rotate pieces with precision, or copy an existing ones rotation
[*][b]Freecam building:[/b] fly the camera around your build and place pieces from there, stays within 25 m of you and the workbench rules still apply, Fly through walls for easier building (optional, off by default)
[*][b]Placement reasons:[/b] tells you why a piece can't be placed, like what it overlaps or that the ground is too steep
[*][b]Crafting stations:[/b] shows the level, which extensions count and which are too far or doubled up
[*]Comfort preview before you place a chair, banner etc.
[*][b]Shelter, support and terrain readouts:[/b] which sides are open, when a piece falls, how deep you can dig
[*][b]Removal lock:[/b] no more deleting your build by accident
[*][b]Hold Left Alt:[/b] snap points, how high the piece sits and which pieces would fall if you removed the one you aim at
[*][b]Layout mode:[/b] drag the panels where you want them
[/list]

[center][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Juissi06/NorseSense/master/art/screenshots/precision.png[/img][/center]

[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Farming, portals and fires[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]Planting preview:[/b] will the seed grow here, and if not, why
[*][b]Area tools:[/b] Left Ctrl + scroll plants a row or grid of seeds in one click, or levels, paths and cultivates a bigger area.
[*]Raise ground in a grid too, it comes out flat, and a 1x3 strip for paths
[*][b]Farm check:[/b] with the cultivator out every crop shows if it's growing and why not
[*][b]Harvest an area:[/b] Left Alt + E picks the whole patch, and replants it if you carry a cultivator and seeds
[*][b]Comfort breakdown while sitting:[/b] what adds comfort and what doesn't count
[*][b]Portals:[/b] where the portal leads, why it isn't connected and the items stopping you from teleporting
[*][b]Fires:[/b] tells you why a fire won't burn
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Cartography table[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Skip crossed-out and duplicate markers when reading a table
[*][b]Left Shift + E:[/b] edit, rename, cross out and delete the table's markers
[/list]

[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Optional(off by default)[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*]Customisable building and planting stamina, longer build distance and pickup range
[*][b]Server limits:[/b] server owners can cap freecam, area sizes, stamina and so on for everyone with the mod
[/list]

[color=#b9ad98]Every feature can be turned on/off and fully tuned.[/color]

[color=#b9ad98]No mod conflicts from my experience, mods changing same things might clash.[/color]

[line]
[size=5][b][color=#e8c77e]Default keys[/color][/b][/size]
[list]
[*][b]F6[/b] removal lock
[*][b]F7[/b] base view (Spawns, Repairs, Comfort)
[*][b]F8[/b] build info
[*][b]F10[/b] freecam building
[*][b]Left Alt + scroll[/b] fine rotation
[*][b]Left Alt + T[/b] match rotation
[*][b]Left Ctrl + scroll[/b] area size (cultivator and hoe)
[*][b]Left Shift + E[/b] at a cartography table
[*][b]Left Ctrl + L[/b] layout mode
[*][b]Hold Left Alt while building:[/b] snap points, height and removal preview
[*][b]Left Alt + E on a crop:[/b] harvest the area
[/list]
