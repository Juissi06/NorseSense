## Description
THIS MOD IS STILL IN ALPHA
Norse Sense shows you the annoying things that Valheim keeps hidden: where mobs can spawn, if raids can reach your base, why your seed won't grow, why your fire won't burn and which item stops you from using a portal. Even has a cartography table manager! Client-side, vanilla-balanced and fully customisable.

Installation:
- Mod managers; automatic install
- Manual: Put the DLL file to \Steam\steamapps\common\Valheim\BepInEx\plugins
- Client-side only, the server doesn't need it

Main features
- Spawn view (F7): shows on the ground and on the map where mobs can and can't spawn, rings around your workbenches and fires, and marks nests and spawners
- Raid check: tells you if raids can target you where you stand
- Fine rotation: Left Alt + scroll rotates pieces in 5° steps, Left Alt + T copies the rotation of the piece you look at
- Comfort preview: see how much comfort you'd get before placing a chair, banner etc.
- Shelter readout: shows if your spot counts as shelter and which sides are still open
- Support readout: exact support number of the piece you look at and when it falls
- Terrain limits: how much more you can dig or raise the ground
- Removal lock (F9): stops the hammer from deleting your build by accident
- Planting preview: shows if the seed will grow where you aim and why not, plus grow space rings
- Portals: lists the items that stop you from teleporting
- Fires: tells you why a fire won't burn or burns low
- Cartography table: skip crossed-out and duplicate markers when reading a table, and edit, rename, cross out and delete the table's markers with Left Shift + E
- Optional: cheaper building/planting stamina, longer build distance and pickup range (off by default)
- Every feature can be turned off, and the whole mod can be switched off in game

Looking for ship stuff like hull health? That's in my sailing mod Seaworthy: <link>

Requirements:
-BepInEx
-Valheim Configuration Manager (optional, but highly recommended for config)
