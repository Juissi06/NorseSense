# Short clips for the mod page

A few seconds each, no HUD clutter, same base so the page feels like one place. Record with the Steam or
Xbox game bar recorder and turn into GIFs (or upload as Nexus videos).

1. **Base view, F7:** stand in your base, press F7 three times: Spawns (red/blue ground, raid line), Repairs
   (tinted pieces), Comfort (floor colours). Shows the main feature in five seconds.
2. **Placing a workbench with the spawn view on:** the cyan preview grows the protected area before you click.
3. **Planting a 3 x 3 grid:** Ctrl + scroll to grow the grid, one click plants nine, rings turn red where a seed won't grow.
4. **Freecam, F10:** fly up over the roof and place a piece from above; bump into a wall to show it stops.
5. **Build info while building:** rotation, shelter "Open behind and to the left", support numbers on a beam.
6. **Comfort card:** sit by the fire, the card fades in with the pieces that count.
7. **Layout mode, Ctrl + L:** drag the legend to the other side of the screen.
8. **Marker manager:** Shift + E on a cartography table, keep one per duplicate group, Save.
